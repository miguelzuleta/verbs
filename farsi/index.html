Persian (Farsi) Verb Tenses

Look up an English verb to see its Persian translation and how to say it in every tense.

Persian is written right to left and leaves out most short vowels, so every form comes with a transliteration underneath. Verbs do not change for gender: the six buttons only change the person. Many verbs are a word plus a light verb, such as قبول کردن (to accept): only the last word is conjugated. A few verbs start with a prefix that stays in front of می‌ and ن (برمی‌دارم). This page uses the formal written style (می‌روم), not everyday speech (میرم). The imperative has only تو, ما ("let's") and شما; the other buttons show the nearest one.

Indicative Past Progressive (ماضی مستمر) داشتم (to have, past) + past continuous داشتم صحبت می‌کردمdâshtam sohbat mikardam An action that was in progress at a past moment
Indicative Present Perfect (ماضی نقلی) Past participle (-ه) + ام / ای / است صحبت کرده‌امsohbat kardeam Past actions connected to the present, or experiences
Indicative Past Perfect (ماضی بعید) Past participle + بودم (to be, past) صحبت کرده بودمsohbat karde budam An action completed before another past action
Indicative Future (آینده) خواهم (to want) + past stem صحبت خواهم کردsohbat khâham kard Actions that will happen later; mostly written (speech uses the present)
Indicative Negative Present (مضارع منفی) نمی‌ + present stem + ending صحبت نمی‌کنمsohbat nemikonam Saying that something does not happen
Indicative Negative Past (ماضی منفی) ن + past stem + ending صحبت نکردمsohbat nakardam Saying that something did not happen
Subjunctive Present Subjunctive (مضارع التزامی) ب + present stem + ending صحبت کنمsohbat konam Wishes, necessity, purpose, what one should do (after words like "that", "so that")
Subjunctive Perfect Subjunctive (ماضی التزامی) Past participle + باشم (to be, subj.) صحبت کرده باشمsohbat karde bâsham Doubt or possibility about a completed action
Imperative Imperative (امر) ب + present stem (+ ید for شما) صحبت کنsohbat kon (you) Telling someone to do something (تو, شما); ما gives "let's"
Imperative Negative Imperative (نهی) ن + present stem (+ ید for شما) صحبت نکنsohbat nakon (you) Telling someone not to do something
