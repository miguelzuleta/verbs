Japanese Verb Forms

Look up an English verb to see its Japanese translation and how to say it in each form.

Japanese verbs have no grammatical person: the same form is used for I, you, he, we and they. The two buttons pick the politeness style, polite (ます style) or plain (dictionary style). Every cell shows the form with its hiragana reading underneath. In the Structure column, "stem" is the part before ます (話し in 話します). Verbs are of two kinds: ichidan (一段) verbs end in an い- or え-row sound plus る and simply drop the る (食べる, 見る), while godan (五段) verbs change their last kana (話す, 買う). A few godan verbs also end in -iru or -eru (帰る, 切る, 入る), so the example row is the safest guide. する and 来る are irregular, and so are a few others (ある has the negative ない; 行く has the te-form 行って).

Plain: te-form + いる		 話していますはなしています Actions in progress, ongoing habits, and states that result from a change

Plain: te-form alone		 話してくださいはなしてください Asking someone to do something; the plain te-form is a casual request

Plain: stem + たい		 話したいですはなしたいです Your own wishes ("I want to ...") and questions to someone ("Do you want to ...?")
Modality Potential (可能形) Plain: last kana changes to its え-row sound, then る (話せる); ichidan: drop る + られる (食べられる)
Polite: the plain form with る→ます		 話せますはなせます Being able to do something ("can")
Voice Passive (受身形) Plain: last kana changes to its あ-row sound, then れる (話される); ichidan: drop る + られる (食べられる)
Polite: the plain form with る→ます		 話されますはなされます Things done to someone, troublesome events that affect them, and respectful speech
Voice Causative (使役形) Plain: last kana changes to its あ-row sound, then せる (話させる); ichidan: drop る + させる (食べさせる)
Polite: the plain form with る→ます		 話させますはなさせます Making or letting someone do something
Volitional Volitional (意向形) Polite: stem + ましょう
Plain: last kana changes to its お-row sound, then う (話そう); ichidan: drop る + よう (食べよう)		 話しましょうはなしましょう "Let's ..." and "I will ..." (invitations and intentions)

Imperative Imperative (命令形) Polite: stem + なさい
Plain: last kana changes to its え-row sound (話せ); ichidan: drop る + ろ (食べろ)		 話しなさいはなしなさい Direct commands; the plain form is blunt, なさい is a firm order from a parent or teacher
