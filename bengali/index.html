Bengali Verb Tenses

Look up an English verb to see its Bengali translation and how to say it in every tense.

Bengali verbs do not change for gender or number. They change for person and for how respectful you are, so each button covers the singular and the plural (আমি / আমরা): তুই is intimate (for close friends and children), তুমি is familiar, আপনি is respectful, and সে / তিনি are the ordinary and the respectful "he / she". Every cell shows the Bengali script with a simplified phonetic romanization underneath: the inherent vowel is written o (করা kora), ছ is chh, and a spoken final o is written (করল korlo). The spelling follows the short style used in West Bengal (করল, করব); in Bangladesh করলো, করবো is common. Compound verbs such as কাজ করা (to work) conjugate on the last word only. The negative past in -নি (করিনি) covers both "did not" and "have not". The imperative has no আমি form, and the negative imperative no ordinary সে form (those buttons show —). Possession, need, obligation and many feelings put the person in the genitive (আমার গাড়ি আছে "I have a car", আমার ভয় লাগে "I am afraid", আমার করা উচিত "I should"); this table always shows the person as the subject. For verbs like যাওয়া and খাওয়া the তুমি negative imperative (যাও না) can sound like urging; the usual prohibition is যেয়ো না, খেয়ো না, which this table does not generate.

Indicative Present (সাধারণ বর্তমান) Stem + -ি / -িস / -ো / -েন / -ে (-i, -is, -o, -en, -e) বলিboli Habits, routines, general facts and what is true now. In everyday speech it is also used for the near future.
Indicative Present Continuous (ঘটমান বর্তমান) Stem + -ছ- (-chh-) + person ending বলছিbolchhi Actions happening right now or around now.
Indicative Simple Past (সাধারণ অতীত) Stem + -লাম / -লি / -লে / -লেন / -ল (-lam, -li, -le, -len, -lo) বললামbollam Completed past actions.
Indicative Past Continuous (ঘটমান অতীত) Stem + -ছিল- (-chhil-) + person ending বলছিলামbolchhilam An action that was in progress at a past moment.
Indicative Past Habitual (নিত্যবৃত্ত অতীত) Stem + -ত- (-t-) + person ending বলতামboltam What used to happen regularly; also the "would" of unreal conditions (জানলে যেতাম "if I had known, I would have gone").
Indicative Present Perfect (পুরাঘটিত বর্তমান) Stem + -েছ- (-echh-) + person ending বলেছিbolechhi Actions completed with a result that still matters now ("I have done it").
Indicative Past Perfect (পুরাঘটিত অতীত) Stem + -েছিল- (-echhil-) + person ending বলেছিলামbolechhilam An action completed before another past action ("I had done it").
Indicative Future (সাধারণ ভবিষ্যৎ) Stem + -ব / -বি / -বে / -বেন (-bo, -bi, -be, -ben) বলবbolbo Actions that will happen later; also polite requests and promises.
Indicative Negative Present (নেতিবাচক বর্তমান) Present form + না (na) after the verb বলি নাboli na Saying that something does not happen or is not happening.
Indicative Negative Past (নেতিবাচক অতীত) Present form + নি (ni) বলিনিbolini Saying that something did not happen or has not happened ("I did not / have not").
Indicative Negative Future (নেতিবাচক ভবিষ্যৎ) Future form + না (na) বলব নাbolbo na Saying that something will not happen.
Imperative Imperative (অনুজ্ঞা) তুই: bare stem; তুমি: -ো; আপনি: -ুন; সে: -ুক — Telling someone to do something; the 3rd-person form means "let him/her/them do it".
Imperative Negative Imperative (নিষেধাজ্ঞা) তুই, তুমি: present form + না; আপনি: future form + না — Telling someone not to do something.
Conditional If / When (শর্তসূচক) Stem + -লে (-le) বললেbolle The conditional participle: "if / when I do ...". The same form for every person.
Verbal forms Verbal Noun (ক্রিয়াবাচক বিশেষ্য) Stem + -া (-a) for most verbs বলাbola The dictionary form and the "-ing" of the verb ("doing", "to do" as a noun).
Verbal forms Infinitive (অসমাপিকা -তে) Stem + -তে (-te) বলতেbolte "To do", "in order to do"; the same form for every person.
Verbal forms Conjunctive (অসমাপিকা -ে) Stem + -ে (-e) for most verbs; a few change the stem (ডেকে, গিয়ে, খেয়ে) বলেbole "Having done", "and then": links an earlier action to the next one.
