Mood Tense (English / German) Structure Example (sprechen) Primary Use
Indicative Present (Präsens) Simple spreche Current actions, habits, general truths; with a time word it also refers to the future
Indicative Perfect (Perfekt) haben or sein (pres.) + participle habe gesprochen The everyday past in speech: completed actions and past events connected to the present
Indicative Simple Past (Präteritum) Simple sprach The written and narrative past (stories, news, reports); in speech mostly for sein, haben and modal verbs
Indicative Past Perfect (Plusquamperfekt) haben or sein (simple past) + participle hatte gesprochen An action completed before another past action
Indicative Future (Futur I) werden (pres.) + infinitive werde sprechen Predictions, promises and plans; also a guess about the present. Everyday speech often uses the present instead
Indicative Future Perfect (Futur II) werden (pres.) + participle + haben or sein werde gesprochen haben An action that will be finished by a future moment; also a guess about the past
Subjunctive Subjunctive I (Konjunktiv I) Simple spreche Reported (indirect) speech, mainly in writing and news: what someone else said
Subjunctive Subjunctive I Perfect (Konjunktiv I Perfekt) haben or sein (subj. I) + participle habe gesprochen Reported speech about something that happened earlier
Subjunctive Subjunctive II (Konjunktiv II) Simple spräche Unreal wishes and conditions ("if I were..."), polite requests; also reported speech when Subjunctive I looks like the indicative
Subjunctive Subjunctive II Pluperfect (Konjunktiv II Plusquamperfekt) haben or sein (subj. II) + participle hätte gesprochen Unreal past situations ("if I had known..."), regrets
Subjunctive Conditional (Konditional I) würde + infinitive würde sprechen The everyday "would": unreal wishes, conditions and polite requests; in speech it replaces Subjunctive II for most verbs
Subjunctive Conditional Perfect (Konditional II) würde + participle + haben or sein würde gesprochen haben Unreal past actions ("would have"); rare, usually replaced by Subjunctive II Pluperfect
Imperative Imperative (Imperativ) Simple sprich (du) Telling someone to do something (only du, wir, ihr and Sie)
