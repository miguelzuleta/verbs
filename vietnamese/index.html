Vietnamese Verb Patterns

Look up an English verb to see its Vietnamese translation and how to express each tense, aspect and mood with it.

Vietnamese verbs never change form. The person only changes the pronoun, and tense comes from particles such as đã (past), đang (in progress), sẽ (future), rồi (already), chưa (not yet) and không (no) placed around the verb. Each row shows one way of expressing a tense or mood. There is no romanization because Vietnamese is already written in the Latin alphabet. The pronouns chosen are the neutral ones (tôi, bạn ...), but real Vietnamese pronouns depend on age and relationship (anh, chị, em, ...). The button anh ấy / cô ấy uses anh ấy in the sentences, which stands for "he" or "she". In commands, "we" is the inclusive chúng ta (you and I), not chúng tôi. Weather verbs such as mưa have no personal subject, so every button shows trời ("the sky"): trời mưa. Where a pattern does not suit a verb the cell shows —.

Mood Tense / Aspect (English / Vietnamese) Structure Example (nói) Primary Use
Indicative Present (Hiện tại) Subject + verb tôi nói Current actions, habits, general truths; no particle is needed
Indicative In Progress (Đang) Subject + đang + verb tôi đang nói Actions happening right now
Indicative Completed (Đã) Subject + đã + verb tôi đã nói Past or completed actions (often left out when a time word such as hôm qua "yesterday" already shows it is past)
Indicative Ever Done (Đã từng) Subject + đã từng + verb tôi đã từng nói Having done something at least once in life
Indicative Already (Rồi) Subject + đã + verb + rồi tôi đã nói rồi Something already done, with a link to the present
Indicative Just Now (Vừa) Subject + vừa + verb tôi vừa nói Something that has just happened
Indicative Past In Progress (Lúc đó … đang) Lúc đó + subject + đang + verb lúc đó tôi đang nói What was happening at a moment in the past
Indicative Past Perfect (Trước đó … đã … rồi) Trước đó + subject + đã + verb + rồi trước đó tôi đã nói rồi An action completed before another moment in the past
Indicative Future (Sẽ) Subject + sẽ + verb tôi sẽ nói Planned or predicted future actions, promises
Indicative About To (Sắp) Subject + sắp + verb tôi sắp nói Something about to happen very soon
Indicative Future Perfect (Đến lúc đó … sẽ … xong) Đến lúc đó + subject + sẽ + verb + xong đến lúc đó tôi sẽ nói xong What will be finished by a future moment (a state or instant verb, or one already ending in xong or hết, uses đã … rồi instead)
Hypothetical Conditional (Nếu … sẽ) Nếu …, subject + sẽ + verb nếu …, tôi sẽ nói What "would" happen if a condition held
Hypothetical Conditional Perfect (Nếu lúc đó … đã … rồi) Nếu lúc đó …, subject + đã + verb + rồi nếu lúc đó …, tôi đã nói rồi What "would have" happened
Hypothetical Hypothetical (Nếu) Nếu + subject + verb nếu tôi nói The "if" clause of a condition ("If I ...")
Hypothetical Counterfactual Past (Giá mà … đã) Giá mà + lúc đó + subject + đã + verb giá mà lúc đó tôi đã nói Past regrets or impossible conditions ("If only I had ...")
Hypothetical Wish (Ước gì) Ước gì + subject + verb ước gì tôi nói Wishes ("I wish ..." / "If only ...")
Imperative Affirmative Imperative (Hãy) subject + hãy + verb! (you, we) / để + subject + verb! (I, he/she, they) để tôi nói! Telling someone to do something; để means "let (me, him, them) ..."; for "we" the command uses the inclusive chúng ta (you and I)
Imperative Negative Imperative (Đừng) subject + đừng + verb! (you, we) / đừng để + subject + verb! (I, he/she, they) đừng để tôi nói! Telling someone not to do something; đừng để means "don't let ..."
Negation Not (Không) Subject + không + verb tôi không nói Present, habitual or future negation
Negation Not Yet (Chưa) Subject + chưa + verb tôi chưa nói Something that has not happened yet
Negation Never (Chưa từng) Subject + chưa từng + verb tôi chưa từng nói Having never done something
Negation Did Not (Đã không) Subject + đã không + verb tôi đã không nói Not doing something in the past (a plain fact or a choice, not "not yet")
Modality Can / Able To (Có thể) Subject + có thể + verb tôi có thể nói Ability or possibility
Modality Should (Nên) Subject + nên + verb tôi nên nói Advice or obligation
Modality Want To (Muốn) Subject + muốn + verb tôi muốn nói Desire or intention
Question Yes/No Question (Có … không?) Subject + có + verb + không? tôi có nói không? Asking a yes/no question (a verb that already begins with có does not take a second one)
Question Done Yet? (Đã … chưa?) Subject + đã + verb + chưa? tôi đã nói chưa? Asking whether something has happened yet; the answers are rồi "already" and chưa "not yet"
