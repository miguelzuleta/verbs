Indicative Imperfect (Imparfait) Simple parlais Ongoing/habitual past actions, descriptions

Indicative Simple Past (Passé simple) Simple parlai Literary; completed past actions in written narrative

Indicative Simple Future (Futur simple) Simple parlerai Actions that will happen later
Indicative Near Future (Futur proche) aller (pres.) + infinitive vais parler The everyday future: what is about to happen or is planned
Indicative Future Perfect (Futur antérieur) avoir or être (fut.) + participle aurai parlé An action that will be finished before another point in the future
Indicative Conditional (Conditionnel présent) Simple parlerais Hypothetical actions ("would" do something), polite requests
Indicative Conditional Perfect (Conditionnel passé) avoir or être (cond.) + participle aurais parlé Hypothetical past actions ("would have"), regrets
Subjunctive Present Subjunctive (Subjonctif présent) Simple parle Doubt, wishes, emotions or necessity (after que)
Subjunctive Past Subjunctive (Subjonctif passé) avoir or être (pres. subj.) + participle aie parlé Doubt or emotion about a completed action
Subjunctive Imperfect Subjunctive (Subjonctif imparfait) Simple parlasse Literary; found only in formal writing
Subjunctive Pluperfect Subjunctive (Subjonctif plus-que-parfait) avoir or être (imp. subj.) + participle eusse parlé Literary; past regrets or impossible past conditions
