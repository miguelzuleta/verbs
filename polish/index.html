Polish Verb Tenses

Look up an English verb to see its Polish translation and every conjugation in the table.

Every Polish verb has an aspect: imperfective (the process of an action, or its repetition) or perfective (a completed result). Most English verbs have one of each, so look at the translation buttons, each marked (impf.) or (pf.). A perfective verb has no present tense (its cells show —) and its simple future uses the present endings. The past tense changes with gender and number, so those forms are shown together as masculine / feminine (and neuter). Reflexive verbs keep się after the verb. Negative commands normally use the imperfective.
