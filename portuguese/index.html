Portuguese Verb Tenses

Look up an English verb to see its Portuguese translation and every conjugation in the table.

This page shows Brazilian Portuguese with the 1990 spelling. The tu and vós forms are shown for completeness, but in everyday Brazilian speech você replaces tu (with the same verb form as ele and ela) and vós is archaic. A dash (—) marks a form that does not exist: weather verbs such as chover only have the ele / ela / você form, and defective verbs such as abolir, banir, colorir, demolir and falir lack the forms that traditional grammar leaves out (in speech many people do use some of them, for example eu coloro).

Indicative Present (Presente) Simple falo Current actions, habits, general truths
Indicative Simple Past (Pretérito Perfeito) Simple falei Completed past actions with a clear start/end
Indicative Imperfect (Pretérito Imperfeito) Simple falava Habits and descriptions in the past; ongoing past actions
Indicative Pluperfect (Pretérito Mais-que-perfeito) Simple falara Literary; an action completed before another past action (the compound form, tinha falado, is the everyday one)
Indicative Future (Futuro do Presente) Simple falarei Actions that will happen later (in speech, ir + infinitive is more common: vou falar)
Indicative Conditional (Futuro do Pretérito) Simple falaria Hypothetical actions ("would" do something), polite requests
Indicative Present Perfect (Pretérito Perfeito Composto) ter (pres.) + participle tenho falado Actions that repeat or continue up to now (tenho falado is "I have been speaking"), not one finished event
Indicative Past Perfect (Pretérito Mais-que-perfeito Composto) ter (imp.) + participle tinha falado The everyday way to say an action was completed before another past action
Indicative Future Perfect (Futuro do Presente Composto) ter (fut.) + participle terei falado Actions that will be finished by a future point
Indicative Conditional Perfect (Futuro do Pretérito Composto) ter (cond.) + participle teria falado Hypothetical past actions ("would have" done)
Subjunctive Present Subjunctive (Presente do Subjuntivo) Simple fale Wishes, doubts or emotions about the present or future (often after que)
Subjunctive Imperfect Subjunctive (Pretérito Imperfeito do Subjuntivo) Simple falasse Past wishes and doubts, unlikely hypotheticals ("If I were...")
Subjunctive Future Subjunctive (Futuro do Subjuntivo) Simple falar Future events after se, quando, assim que ("if you speak", "when you speak")
Subjunctive Perfect Subjunctive (Pretérito Perfeito do Subjuntivo) ter (pres. subj.) + participle tenha falado Doubt or emotion about a completed action ("I hope you have...")
Subjunctive Pluperfect Subjunctive (Pretérito Mais-que-perfeito do Subjuntivo) ter (imp. subj.) + participle tivesse falado Past regrets or impossible past conditions ("If I had...")
Subjunctive Future Perfect Subjunctive (Futuro Composto do Subjuntivo) ter (fut. subj.) + participle tiver falado An action that will be finished before another future point, after se, quando, assim que
Imperative Imperative (Imperativo Afirmativo) Simple (você, nós and vocês use subj. forms) fala (tu) Telling someone to do something
Imperative Negative Imperative (Imperativo Negativo) não + present subjunctive não fales (tu) Telling someone not to do something
Infinitive Personal Infinitive (Infinitivo Pessoal) Simple (infinitive + -es, -mos, -des, -em) falar An infinitive that agrees with its subject, e.g. para falares ("for you to speak")
