Korean Verb Forms

Look up an English verb to see its Korean translation and how to say it in each form.

Korean verbs have no grammatical person: the same form serves for I, you, he and they. The three buttons pick the speech level instead: 해요체 (polite, everyday), 합쇼체 (formal) and 해체 (casual). Every cell shows the Hangul with its romanization underneath. The Structure column lists the endings in that order, separated by dots; forms that link clauses or describe a noun are the same at every level.
