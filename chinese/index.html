Traditional Chinese Verb Patterns

Look up an English verb to see its Traditional Chinese translation (Mandarin, Taiwan) and how to express each tense, aspect and mood with it.

Chinese verbs never change form. The person only changes the pronoun, and tense comes from words like 了, 過 and 會 placed around the verb.
