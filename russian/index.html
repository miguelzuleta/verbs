Russian Verb Tenses

Look up an English verb to see its Russian translation and every conjugation in the table.

Every Russian verb has an aspect: imperfective (the process of an action, or its repetition) or perfective (a completed result). Most English verbs have one of each, so look at the translation buttons, each marked (impf.) or (pf.). A perfective verb has no present tense (its cells show —) and its simple future uses the present endings. The past tense changes with gender (masculine / feminine / neuter) and number, so those forms are shown together. The stress mark appears on the headword only. Negative imperatives are normally imperfective.
